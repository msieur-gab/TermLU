# Answers

Words for the conversation itself, outside any skill.

- yes: yes, yeah, yep, sure, ok, okay, go ahead, do it, please do, approve, correct
- no: no, nope, don't, do not, not now, stop
- cancel: cancel, never mind, nevermind, forget it, abort
- help: help, what can you do, what do you do, what can i ask
- filler start: please, can you, could you, would you, will you, hey, ok, okay, i want to, i'd like to, i need to
- filler end: please, for me, now, thanks, thank you
- not a name start: in, inside, into, to, on, at, under, from, for, with, of, by
- before a name: call it, name it, let's call it, called, named, titled, it's called, the name is
- pointing words: this, that, it, these, those, here, there
- small words: the, a, an, my, me, i, i'm, it, it's, is, are, am, be, to, of, in, on, at, for, with, from, and, or, please, can, could, would, you, do, does, what, what's, which, how, how's, there, this, that, some, any, up, now, right, still, just, all, so, again, back, get, got, have, has, need, want, let, let's, us, we, will, should, much, very, really, bit, little, too

## Replies

- abstain: I can't do that yet. | I don't know how to do that yet.
- admin off: That needs admin mode, which is off. You can turn it on in your settings.
- declined: OK, I won't. | Fine, nothing done.
- cancelled: OK, cancelled. | Cancelled.
- choose: Do you mean {options}?
- unknown value: I don't see {value}. Which one?
- already: There's already a {file} in {folder}. | {folder} already has a {file}.
- elsewhere: {file} isn't in {here}, but there's one in {folder}. That one?
- several: There are {count} files called {file}, in {folders}. Which one?
- not found: I can't find {file} in {here}, or anywhere in your home folder.
- did you mean: Did you mean {value}? | {value}, right?
- help: I can: {skills}.
- not sure: I don't know {words} yet. Do you want me to {label}? | I haven't learned {words} yet. Should I {label}?
- new words: I don't know {words} yet.
- confirm: Do you want me to {label}? | Should I {label}?
- sounds like: That sounds like "{label}", which needs admin mode. You can turn it on in your settings.
