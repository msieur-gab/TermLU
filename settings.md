# Settings

Each person sets here how much TermNL may do on this device.
The chat page has switches for "show commands" and "admin mode"; they are written back here.

name:
agent name:
language: en
show commands: on
admin mode: off

## For this device

home: ~/TermNL-home
simulate: restart system, shut down system, turn on wifi, turn off wifi, turn on bluetooth, turn off bluetooth, restart service

`home` is the folder that "home" means in every request: files and folders are made there.
`simulate` lists skills (by their `does:`) that never run here: TermNL says what it would have done.
