# Actions

What to do. Each line: the action, then the words people use for it.
A skill names its action in `does:`; its sentences write it as `<action>`.

- check: check, test, verify
- show: show, show me, list, display
- find: find, look for, locate, search for
- create: create, make, add, new, start
- open: open, launch, start, run, bring up
- turn on: turn on, switch on, enable, activate
- turn off: turn off, switch off, disable, deactivate, shut off
- restart: restart, reboot, relaunch, reload
- shut down: shut down, shutdown, power off, power down
- leave: leave, exit, get out of
- rename: rename, change the name of
- close: close, quit
- play: play, put on, listen to
- pause: pause
- stop: stop
- next: next, skip
- previous: previous
- mute: mute, silence
- unmute: unmute

## Meaning with an object

Some words mean another action with some objects: "turn on the music" plays it, "stop the wifi" turns it
off, "turn off the sound" mutes it. Each line: the objects, then the words and the action they mean
there, after an arrow. A word only changes its meaning when one of these objects is in the sentence.

- music: turn on, switch on, start → play
- music, track: turn off, switch off, shut off, end → stop
- wifi, bluetooth: start, bring up, put on → turn on
- wifi, bluetooth: stop, kill, cut, shut off, disconnect, close → turn off
- sound: turn on, switch on, bring back → unmute
- sound: turn off, switch off, cut, kill → mute
- service: bounce, kick → restart
