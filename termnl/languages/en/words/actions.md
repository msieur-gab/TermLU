# Actions

What to do. Each line: the action, then the words people use for it.
A skill names its action in `does:`; its sentences write it as `<action>`.

- check: check, test, verify
- show: show, show me, list, display
- find: find, look for, locate, search for
- create: create, make, add, new, start
- open: open, launch, start, run, bring up
- turn on: turn on, switch on, enable, activate
- turn off: turn off, switch off, disable, deactivate, shut off
- restart: restart, reboot, relaunch, reload
- shut down: shut down, shutdown, power off, power down
- leave: leave, exit, get out of
- rename: rename, change the name of
- close: close, quit
- play: play, put on, listen to
- pause: pause
- stop: stop
- next: next, skip
- previous: previous
- mute: mute, silence
- unmute: unmute
