# Readings

The words shown with what TermNL reads on the device: table headings, the names of rows, and the small
phrases around numbers. The name before the colon is the reader's own and stays as it is; translate what
follows it. `{n}`, `{used}`… are filled in with the numbers.

## Sizes and time

- units: B, KB, MB, GB, TB
- day: {n} day
- days: {n} days
- hour: {n} hour
- hours: {n} hours
- minute: {n} minute
- minutes: {n} minutes
- and: {a} and {b}
- today: today {time}
- date: {day} {month} {year}
- months: Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec
- unknown: unknown

## The clock

- clock time: {hour}:{minute}
- clock date: {day} {month} {year}
- weekdays: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday
- months in full: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December
